**LEBRON JAMES**

He really can't act. This movie is unapologetic WB corporate self-fellatio with hilariously empty dialogue. The familial conflict and hollow sentiment made the movie even worse. The only real saving grace for this movie was the actual Looney Tunes cartoon sequences, which was then squandered by the random conversion to CGI for the main game. That was just offensive. The fact they were able to stretch this out to 2 hours is a miracle. I really went from watching a sentimental masterpiece in Wild Strawberries to this steaming pile of shit. Take all of the hallmarks of a soulless cash grab and mix it in with terrible costumes and awful CGI and you get this. The only reason this didn't get 1/2 a star is because it was entertaining to rip apart.
