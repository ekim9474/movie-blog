**DID NOT FINISH**

I know, I shouldn't have expected anything out of a modern Steven Seagal movie. Even with the lowest of expectations, it was somehow worse than I could have ever imagined. Out for a Kill moves at such a nauseating pace with nonsensical cuts, with a cacophony of transitional noises and monotone line deliveries. It literally took all of a few minutes to make me feel like I had a lobotomy, hell, I would have taken a drill through my brain over continuing this movie. I got about 30-40 minutes in and could not stand it any longer. It's funny cause I love shitty movies, but this was too much to handle.
